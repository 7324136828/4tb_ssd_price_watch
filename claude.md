4TB SSD Price Watch

About this log
A cloud routine checks prices every day at 9:00 AM Eastern and adds a dated entry to the Price log below, newest first.
• Fits: Acer Nitro V 16S (ANV16S-71). M.2 2280, NVMe, PCIe 4.0, no heatsink.
• Tracked (five categories): (1) ssd: 4 TB M.2 2280 NVMe PCIe 4.0 drives without heatsink (Lexar NM790, TeamGroup MP44 and T-Force G50, WD Black SN7100 and SN850X, WD Blue SN5000, Samsung 990 EVO Plus and 990 Pro, Crucial T500 and P310, Kingston KC3000, Silicon Power UD90, other reputable brands), plus the Micron 2500 at 4 TB, or 2 TB if no 4 TB version exists; (2) ram: single 64 GB DDR5 SO-DIMM modules, non-ECC, 5600 MT/s or faster; (3) nvidia_gpu: desktop RTX 3090, 3090 Ti, 4090 and 5090, cheapest new and cheapest used/refurbished; (4) amd_gpu: desktop RX 9070 XT, RX 9070, RX 7900 XTX and RX 7900 XT (plus any newer Radeon above the 9070 XT), cheapest new and used/refurbished; (5) egpu: Thunderbolt/USB4 eGPU enclosures and complete external GPUs (no OCuLink-only or proprietary docks).
• Stores: Amazon, eBay, Newegg, Best Buy, Walmart, B&H Photo, Micro Center (US, USD). eBay used and refurbished listings appear in their own table.
• DEAL flag: a new 4 TB TLC SSD from a reputable brand at $450 or less; or, in any category, an item at least 10% below its most recent earlier price in prices.csv (same item, store and condition), or strictly below every earlier price for that item once prices.csv has at least 7 days of history.
